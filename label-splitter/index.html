How it works

Each page of a Flipkart shipping PDF has a shipping label on top and a tax invoice below a dashed cut line. This tool crops the label to a clean 4×6 inch page (one per parcel) and arranges the invoices several‑to‑a‑page to save paper. Everything runs in your browser — no file ever leaves your device.
